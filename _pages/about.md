Welcome to my website! I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the [Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research (PIK)](https://www.pik-potsdam.de/en), where I specialize in political behavior and public opinion. My research examines trust in institutions, democratic attitudes, and risk perceptions, including how these are shaped by public policy in the areas of the environment and development. My work has been published in journals such as Local Government Studies, Environmental Science & Policy, and International Political Science Review. I hold a PhD in Political Science from the University of Colorado Boulder.

Prior to my PhD, my academic and professional training was in climate and environmental policy. I hold a Master's degree in Climate Science and Policy from TERI University (now the TERI School of Advanced Studies). From 2016 to 2019, I worked as a Research Associate at The Energy and Resources Institute (TERI) in New Delhi, where I focused on issues related to climate change vulnerability and adaptation. This work took me to several field sites across India, particularly in the Himalayan states of Uttarakhand and Sikkim. I am indebted to these communities for their time and for shaping my research. 

Beyond research, I love travelling and photography (go check-out my [Photos](/photos/)!).
